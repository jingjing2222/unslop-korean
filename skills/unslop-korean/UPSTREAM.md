Changes in this adaptation:

- Keep the screen audit, product direction and nine ordered design passes.
- Replace English copy rules, copy signs and final copy checks with the existing
  Korean criteria for contextual meaning, expression and reader understanding.
  These apply within the original Copy pass, without a second workflow or a
  mandatory structured audit report.
- Remove case conversion as a text edit; check Korean glyph coverage and wrapping
  while preserving other-language text. The Latin font table remains a visual reference.
- Route copy-only requests directly to Korean review. Design suggestions cannot
  expand language-editing permission or erase necessary information.
- Keep the upstream Node/Playwright snapshot and video tools unchanged.
- Retain the upstream reference images as visual examples, not Korean prose evidence.

Full Korean source evidence is maintained in the repository's `docs/evidence/`;
the Korean criteria link directly to that preserved evidence.
